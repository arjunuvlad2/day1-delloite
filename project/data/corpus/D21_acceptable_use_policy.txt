Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) v3.3

Company equipment and accounts are provided for business use. Reasonable personal use is tolerated if it does not interfere with work, break the law or create risk.

You must NOT:
 - install unapproved software or browser extensions (request via Software Catalogue)
 - connect unknown USB drives
 - share your credentials with anyone, including IT (we will never ask)
 - forward client documents to personal email or personal cloud storage
 - access, store or circulate offensive, discriminatory or illegal content
 - use company systems for outside business activities or crypto mining
 - circumvent security tools (proxy, DLP, endpoint agent)

Monitoring: Northwind logs network, email metadata and endpoint activity for security and compliance. Content inspection is applied only on DLP rule matches or during an authorised investigation.

Social media: do not share confidential info; don't imply you speak for Northwind unless authorised.

By logging in you accept this policy. Violations -> HR + Security review.
