POL-HR-004 - Annual leave (v2.2)

Full-time staff receive 25 days annual leave per leave year (1 Jan - 31 Dec), plus public holidays for the country of their contract. Part-timers pro rata.

Carry-over: up to 5 days may be carried to 31 March of the following year; anything above that is forfeited unless HR approved an exception because of client delivery pressure (document in Workday).

Booking rules
- 2+ weeks in a row: give your manager 4 weeks notice
- Peak periods (Dec year-end close, late Mar): engagement partner sign-off needed
- You cannot "sell" leave back to the firm, except on termination where accrued untaken days are paid out.

Long-service: +1 day at 5 yrs, +2 days at 10 yrs (capped at 30).

Public holidays falling on weekends are NOT moved to Monday unless the local statutory rule says so.

Pls book leave in Workday, not by email -- email requests are not tracked and cause payroll mismatches every year!
