OFFICES & FACILITIES FAQ

Opening hours: reception 08:00-18:00 Mon-Fri. Badge access 06:30-22:00. Weekend access needs a request to Facilities 24h ahead.

Visitors: register in the Visitor app at least 1 hour before arrival; they must be escorted at all times and wear a visitor badge. Visitors can't use the staff Wi-Fi - guest network only.

Desks: hot-desking, book through the Desk app. Bookings not checked in within 30 minutes are released.

Parking: limited bays; waitlist via Facilities. Bicycle storage and showers in London, Manchester, Leeds.

Catering: tea, coffee and fruit free. Lunch not provided. Client catering must be ordered 48 h ahead via the catering portal.

Printing: print-to-card at any floor printer. Confidential documents should use secure release and shouldn't be left on the tray.

First aid: marked boxes on each floor; first-aiders are listed on the digital signage.

Lost property: reception holds items for 30 days.

Problems with temperature, lighting, broken chairs? Facilities Helpdesk ext. 4444 or facilities@northwind-advisory.example.
