HERON - retro notes (Feb 2024)

Client: regional retail bank. 9-month data quality & lineage programme supporting regulatory reporting.

Wins
- Agreeing 30 "critical data elements" up-front gave a finite scope.
- Automated rule library (180 DQ rules in a catalog) reused across 6 source systems.
- Weekly "data clinic" with business owners moved fixes from IT to the people who understand the data.

Pains
- Lineage tool licences arrived 10 weeks late (procurement of the vendor tool at the client). We hand-drew lineage in the interim and had to redo it.
- Business glossary definitions were contested (what is an "active customer"?). Needed a CDO decision; took 6 weeks.
- Rule thresholds set too tight produced alert fatigue: 4,000 exceptions per day, nobody read them.

Do differently
* Check dependencies on client-side procurement in week 1 and put them on the RAID log
* Make a decision-log with named decision makers and deadlines
* Start with thresholds based on observed distribution, tighten gradually
* Show quick wins by month 2 (we only delivered in month 4)

Team morale was good; two of the juniors have been promoted.
- Meera (Heron PM)
