Internal guidance for category managers. CONFIDENTIAL - includes names of vendors under enhanced monitoring.

TIER 1 (Critical): outage or breach would stop client delivery or expose client personal data.
  Examples: cloud hosting provider, identity provider, e-mail/collaboration suite, payroll provider.
  Controls: annual on-site or virtual audit, SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 report reviewed by InfoSec, exit plan, quarterly service review, cyber insurance verified.
TIER 2 (Significant): important but replaceable within weeks. Examples: expense tool, survey platform, translation agency.
  Controls: annual questionnaire, report review on request.
TIER 3 (Low): no access to data or systems. Stationery, catering, window cleaning.
  Controls: onboarding checks only.

Enhanced monitoring list (Q2 2024): two payroll sub-processors and a legacy file-transfer vendor flagged after financial downgrade. Don't discuss outside Procurement/InfoSec.

Re-tiering triggers: change of data scope, merger/acquisition of the vendor, security incident, spend > 3x contract value.
Fourth-party (vendor's vendors) concentration risk: we map AWS/Azure dependencies for Tier 1 only.
