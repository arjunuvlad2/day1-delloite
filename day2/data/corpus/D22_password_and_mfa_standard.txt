POL-SEC-012  Passwords & MFA  (v3.1)

Minimum requirements:
* Length: 14+ characters (passphrases encouraged); no complexity rules, no forced rotation unless compromise is suspected (aligned with NIST 800-63B)
* Banned list: top 100k breached passwords, company name, season+year patterns
* No re-use across systems; use the approved password manager (Northwind Vault)
* Service accounts: 32+ random characters stored in the secrets vault, rotated every 90 days

MFA
- Mandatory for email, VPN, Workday, admin consoles and all client portals.
- Preferred factor: authenticator app push with number matching. SMS is permitted only as break-glass and is being retired by Dec 2025.
- Hardware security keys (FIDO2) required for privileged admins.

Lockout: 10 failed attempts -> 15 min lock. Unlock through self-service or Service Desk after identity verification.

Exceptions: only via the Risk Exception Register, time-limited, CISO sign-off.

Reminder: Service Desk will never ask for your MFA code. If someone does -> report it as phishing.
