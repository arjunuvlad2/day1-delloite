POL-HR-022  v2.1  (effective 1 June 2022)

After the pandemic restrictions we're moving to a "2 + 3" hybrid: a minimum of TWO days in the office each week, remainder at home or other agreed place.

No fixed anchor days - teams decide. Hot-desk booking via the Desk app is mandatory; if there is no desk, you may stay home.

Client-site staff follow client rules.

Home-office allowance: GBP 150 one-off.

Occasional working from a cafe or co-working space is fine so long as you keep client data secure (see InfoSec policy).

NB: attendance is NOT monitored. Managers should trust their teams.
