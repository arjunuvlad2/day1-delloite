POL-PRO-003  Third-party onboarding  (v2.0)

No supplier may be paid until it exists in the vendor master (SAP Ariba). Do not issue work orders or sign contracts before onboarding completes.

Steps
1. Business sponsor raises a Supplier Request in Ariba with: legal name, registration number, purpose, estimated annual spend, data involved (none / internal / client / personal)
2. Procurement screens against sanctions and adverse-media lists (2-3 working days)
3. Risk tier is assigned (see Vendor Risk Tiering): Tier 1 critical, Tier 2 significant, Tier 3 low
4. Tier 1 and 2: security questionnaire (SIG-Lite), financial health check, data-processing agreement where personal data is involved, insurance certificates
5. Contract review by Legal using the standard MSA template; deviations need Legal sign-off
6. Bank details verified by call-back to a number NOT supplied in the email (fraud control)
7. Vendor activated; sponsor notified

Typical lead time: Tier 3 - 5 days; Tier 2 - 3 weeks; Tier 1 - 6-8 weeks.

Sole-source justification required if spend > GBP 50k without competitive quotes.
Subcontractors who touch client data must be disclosed to the client under most of our MSAs - flag early!
