POL-RISK-002 - Accepting new work (v4)

No work (including 'free pilots' or workshops) may start before the engagement is accepted in the Acceptance & Continuance (A&C) tool.

Required before acceptance
1. Client due diligence: identity, ownership, sanctions & PEP screening (KYC)
2. Independence check: does Northwind or any partner have a financial interest, audit relationship or advisory conflict? Audit clients restrict many advisory services.
3. Conflict search across the engagement database for adverse parties or competing mandates
4. Risk rating (Low / Medium / High) based on sector, geography, contract terms, liability cap, reliance on AI or novel tech
5. Engagement letter reviewed by Legal when liability is uncapped, indemnities requested, or fees contingent on results

Approvals: Low - engagement partner; Medium - + Risk Partner; High - + Regional Managing Partner.
Typical turnaround 3 days (High risk up to 10).

Changes of scope above 20% of fees require a re-check.

Declined opportunities are recorded with reason (good for pipeline analytics).
