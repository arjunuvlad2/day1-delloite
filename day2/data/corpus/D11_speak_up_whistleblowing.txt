POL-ETH-001 Speak Up policy

If you see or suspect conduct that is illegal, dishonest, harassing, discriminatory or contrary to our Code of Conduct, we want to hear about it. Raising a concern in good faith will never be held against you.

Channels (pick whichever feels safest)
1. Your line manager or engagement leader
2. Your People Partner
3. The Ethics & Compliance Office - ethics@northwind-advisory.example
4. The independent Speak Up Line, run by an external provider: online form or phone, 24/7, anonymous if you wish, available in 30+ languages

What happens next: acknowledgement within 3 working days; triage by the Ethics Panel (never involving anyone named in the report); investigation led by someone independent of the business line. Outcome is shared with the reporter where legally possible.

Retaliation is itself a disciplinary matter, up to dismissal.

Anonymous reports are taken seriously but are harder to investigate - try to include dates, names, and where evidence might be found.

This policy covers client matters too: if a client asks you to do something that feels wrong (e.g. backdate documents, hide a finding), stop and call the Speak Up Line.
