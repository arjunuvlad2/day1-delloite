Q: When is the review cycle?
A: Mid-year check-in in July, year-end review in Dec/Jan. Calibration sessions happen in Jan for Managers and above.

Q: What are the rating bands?
A: 1 = Exceptional, 2 = Strong, 3 = Solid, 4 = Developing, 5 = Not meeting expectations. We do NOT force a distribution but calibration will challenge too many 1s.

Q: Who writes my feedback?
A: You (self-assessment), your counselor (career coach), and 3-5 peers you nominate. Engagement leaders on projects >8 weeks add project feedback in the tool.

Q: Does it affect my bonus?
A: Yes - bonus pool allocation uses the calibrated rating, but also firm performance. Details go out in Feb.

Q: I disagree with my rating.
A: Raise it with your counselor within 10 working days; escalate to your People Partner if unresolved. Formal grievance remains available under the Grievance procedure.

Q: Promotion?
A: Promotion cases are submitted separately in March & September.
