Policy ID: POL-HR-017  |  Version 3.1  |  Effective 15 Jan 2022  |  Owner: HR

Purpose: to set out leave entitlements for employees who become parents.

Entitlement (v3.1)
 * Primary carer - 16 weeks at full pay
 * Secondary carer - 2 weeks at full pay
 * Adoption - same as birth for primary carer
 * Additional unpaid leave may be requested, subject to manager approval, up to 12 months.

Eligibility: employees with 6 months' continuous service (probationary staff excluded).

Application: complete the paper form HR-22 and send to your HRBP. Notice of 8 weeks before due date is expected.

Return to work: no phased return option, employees return full-time. Please discuss flexible working with your manager informally.

This policy is reviewed every 3 yrs. Next review due Jan 2025.
