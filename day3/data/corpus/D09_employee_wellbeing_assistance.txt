We're all human, and consulting can be relentless. Northwind provides free, confidential support to every colleague and their household members.

Available 24/7 via the Northwind Assist line (provided by an independent third party - we never see names or details):
 - Short-term counselling: up to 8 sessions per issue, per year, face to face or video
 - Stress, exhaustion and overload coaching
 - Financial and legal first-line advice
 - Bereavement and carer support

Also available:
 * Mental Health First Aiders in every office (look for the green lanyard)
 * Quarterly "no-meeting Friday" afternoons
 * Subsidised gym and mindfulness app (Calm) - claim via benefits portal

Signs you or a teammate might need help: persistent tiredness, withdrawing from the team, irritability, missing deadlines that normally wouldn't be missed, working through the night regularly.

You don't need your manager's permission to use the service, and using it is never recorded in your HR file.

Phone: 0800-555-0142 (UK) / see intranet for other countries.
