PM-2024-02  Credential phishing (fake "shared document" notification)
Severity: SEV-3 (no confirmed data loss)   Prepared by: SOC (D. Mensah)

What happened
On Tue 16 Jan 2024 at 08:12, 212 employees received an email styled as a SharePoint document-share from a known client domain (compromised third-party mailbox). 41 clicked, 19 entered credentials into a lookalike login page. The SOC's mail-gateway rule caught the pattern at 08:47 and purged the remaining copies.

Response
- 19 accounts reset and sessions revoked within 25 minutes of detection
- Conditional-access logs reviewed: 2 sign-in attempts from an overseas IP were blocked by MFA number-matching
- Client notified the same day that their mailbox was compromised
- Domain of the lookalike page submitted for takedown

Why it worked (partly)
MFA prevented any account takeover. But 19 people typed their password into a fake site, which is a failure of awareness, not of technology.

Actions
1. Roll out the "Report Phish" button to mobile mail
2. Add an external-sender banner that cannot be hidden
3. Targeted refresher training for the 41 clickers (non-punitive)
4. Move from SMS fallback to app-only MFA for all staff (see POL-SEC-012)
5. Phishing simulation cadence from quarterly to monthly

Metric to watch: click rate in simulations (target < 5%; Jan 2024 actual 19%).
