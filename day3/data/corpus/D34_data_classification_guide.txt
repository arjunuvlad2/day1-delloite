source: SharePoint/InfoSec/Standards
department: Security
date: 2023-08-15
sensitivity: public

Northwind uses three labels. When in doubt, go UP one level.

PUBLIC - already published or approved for release: marketing brochures, job adverts, press releases, public thought leadership.

INTERNAL - default for everything created at work: policies, how-tos, org charts, meeting notes, project status that doesn't name a client's confidential numbers. Fine to share with employees and vetted contractors. Not for the internet.

CONFIDENTIAL - disclosure could harm clients, colleagues or the firm: client deliverables and datasets, personal data of individuals, HR case files, financial results before release, security incident reports, pricing and bids, board papers, vulnerability details.
  Handling: encrypted storage, need-to-know access, label applied in the document header or file properties, no printing without reason, share only through approved platforms (SharePoint with restricted groups, secure transfer).

Examples
 - Anonymised summary statistics -> Internal
 - Raw customer survey export with email addresses -> Confidential
 - Post-mortem for an outage -> Confidential until the actions are closed, then Internal if scrubbed of client names

Labels in M365 drive DLP and the search system's security trimming: the label is metadata and must be right.
