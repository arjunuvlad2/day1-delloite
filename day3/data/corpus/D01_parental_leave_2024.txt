1. Purpose
Northwind wants every colleague to be able to welcome a child without worrying about the pay cheque. This policy replaces v3.1 (Jan 2022) in full.

2. Eligibility
All permanent and fixed-term employees from day one of employment. Contractors are not covered (see your agency).

3. Entitlement
- Primary carer: 26 weeks at full base salary
- Secondary carer / partner: 12 weeks at full base salary
- Adoption and surrogacy: treated identically to birth
- Stillbirth or loss after 20 weeks: same entitlement as above, no questions asked

Leave may be taken in up to two blocks within the first 18 months. A "keeping in touch" arrangement (max 10 days) is possible only if the employee requests it in writing.

4. How to apply
Tell your line manager at least 15 weeks before the expected due date where possible, then submit the form in Workday > Absence > Family Leave. HR Ops confirms within 5 working days.

5. Return to work
Phased return (80% for 4 weeks, pay stays at 100%) is available. Talk to your People Partner.
