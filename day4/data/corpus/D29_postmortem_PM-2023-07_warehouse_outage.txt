POST-MORTEM  PM-2023-07
Incident: Analytics data warehouse unavailable for 6h 40m
Date: Saturday 9 September 2023   Severity: SEV-2   Author: Lena Fischer (Platform Lead)   Status: Final, blameless format

SUMMARY
During a scheduled storage-tier migration, the production analytics warehouse (used by 14 client dashboards and the internal utilisation reports) stopped accepting queries at 02:10 UTC and was fully restored at 08:50 UTC. No data was lost. Two clients (a retailer and a utilities client) saw stale dashboards on a Monday-morning board pack because the refresh jobs backed up.

IMPACT
- 14 client dashboards stale or erroring
- ~35 consultants unable to run ad-hoc queries until Sunday afternoon
- One contractual SLA breach (99.5% monthly availability, measured 99.1% for the retailer)

TIMELINE (UTC)
02:10 Migration job begins moving cold partitions to the cheaper storage tier.
02:31 Query latency climbs; auto-scaling adds nodes, which makes it worse (more nodes -> more metadata locks).
03:05 First alert fires, but it was routed to a distribution list that nobody had been watching since the January team reorganisation.
04:40 A consultant in Singapore raises a ticket manually. On-call engineer paged.
05:20 Root cause hypothesis: metadata lock contention.
06:15 Migration job killed; locks persist.
07:30 Decision to restart the metadata service in a controlled fashion.
08:50 Service restored; backlog drained by 11:30.

ROOT CAUSE
The migration was run during the weekend batch window while three heavy ETL jobs were also scheduled. The migration tool takes a table-level lock per partition batch and our auto-scaling policy reacted to latency by adding nodes rather than shedding load.

CONTRIBUTING FACTORS
1. Alert routing was stale after a reorganisation.
2. No runbook entry for "metadata lock storm".
3. Change was not peer-reviewed against the batch schedule.
4. Client comms were sent 3.5 hours after the first alert.

WHAT WENT WELL
On-call handover was clean; backups were verified and we did not need to restore from them.

ACTION ITEMS
- AI-1 Fix alert routing and add quarterly alert-ownership audit (Owner: Platform, due 30 Sep 2023) - DONE
- AI-2 Write runbook for lock contention (Owner: Lena, due 15 Oct) - DONE
- AI-3 Change calendar must show ETL schedule; block overlapping heavy changes (Owner: Change Mgr) - DONE
- AI-4 Cap auto-scaling at 1.5x baseline during maintenance - IN PROGRESS
- AI-5 Client-comms template: first update within 60 minutes of SEV-2 declaration

LESSON FOR ARCHITECTS
Resilience is not only redundancy: look at what your automatic reactions do under contention. Retries and autoscaling can amplify failure.
