POL-PRIV-002 - Individuals' rights requests (v2.3)

Who this is for: anyone who receives a message from a member of the public, a client's employee, a candidate or a colleague asking what Northwind holds about them, asking for corrections, or asking us to erase or restrict use of their information.

Rules
1. Do not reply on your own. Forward within ONE business day to privacy@northwind-advisory.example and log the receipt date. The statutory clock (one month under GDPR/UK GDPR, 45 days under CCPA) starts when we receive it, not when Privacy does.
2. Privacy verifies the identity of the requester (never ask for more ID than needed).
3. Privacy determines our role. If Northwind is the PROCESSOR for a client, the request is passed to the client (the controller) - we must not fulfil it directly unless the contract says so.
4. Search across systems: HR (Workday), CRM, mailboxes, shared drives, backups (flagged for exclusion, not wiped).
5. Exemptions: legal hold, regulatory retention obligations, legal privilege, others' rights. Erasure is not absolute.
6. Response pack approved by Privacy Counsel.

Typical request types: access (SAR), rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), restriction, portability, objection to marketing.

Turnaround target: 20 days internally to leave slack.
Tip: a client's staff member emailing you directly on a project is still a request. Same rules.
