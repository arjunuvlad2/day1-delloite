Hi Marcus,
Approval limits (delegation of authority, DoA matrix v6):
 - up to GBP 1,000: your line manager (corporate card OK)
 - GBP 1,001 - 10,000: Associate Director + budget holder; raise a PO in Ariba
 - GBP 10,001 - 50,000: Director and Finance Business Partner, 3 quotes
 - above GBP 50,000: Practice Lead + CFO delegate, plus sole-source form if no competition
Software also needs an InfoSec review before purchase (the "Shadow IT" check) - raise a ticket under Software Request, takes 5 days. Don't skip that or the PO will bounce.
So you need AD + budget holder + InfoSec. Recurring subscriptions count at annual value.
