POL-PRIV-005  Retention schedule (v3.0)

Rule of thumb: keep it as long as you must, and delete it when you may.

Record class                                     Retained for                 Trigger
Engagement working papers & deliverables         7 years                     Engagement closure
Client contracts and SOWs                         7 years                     Contract end
Financial / tax records                           7 years (HMRC: 6 + current) End of financial year
Personnel files                                   6 years                     Employment end
Recruitment - unsuccessful candidates             12 months                   Decision date
CCTV and badge logs                               90 days                     Capture
Mailbox (general)                                 3 years rolling              Receipt
Teams chats                                       2 years rolling              Message date
Security logs                                     13 months                   Capture
Marketing consent records                         Life of consent + 3 years    -

Overrides
- Legal hold suspends all deletion for the named matter. Legal issues & lifts holds, nobody else.
- Client contracts can require shorter periods (return/destroy at end) or longer; the engagement manager records the exception in the closure checklist.
- Personal data: do not retain beyond the purpose even if schedule maximum not yet reached.

Disposal: automated for SharePoint/Exchange labels; manual certificate of destruction for paper and removable media (via the secure shred bins).
Do not store working copies on personal drives - they won't be reached by the disposal run.
