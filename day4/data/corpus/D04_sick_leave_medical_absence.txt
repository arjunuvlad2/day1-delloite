POL-HR-009 Sick leave (v1.4)

If you're unwell and can't work:
1. Tell your manager before your normal start time (Teams message is fine, phone for client-site staff).
2. Log the absence in Workday on the first day.
3. Self-certification covers up to 5 consecutive working days. From day 6 a fit note (doctor's certificate) is required.

Pay: full pay for up to 30 working days in any rolling 12 months, then 50% for up to 60 further days. Long-term conditions are handled via an Occupational Health referral - ask your People Partner, nobody will judge you.

Medical info is confidential: managers see dates only, never diagnosis.

Returning after 10+ days: short return-to-work chat, not a disciplinary meeting.
Reasonable adjustments (ergonomic chair, screen reader, reduced hours) can be requested at any time via the Workplace Adjustments form.

If you get sick while on annual leave abroad, send proof & the days convert to sick leave.
