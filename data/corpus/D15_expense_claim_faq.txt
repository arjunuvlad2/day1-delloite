Expense FAQ  (updated Sept 2024 by Finance Ops)

Q. My claim is "Pending manager" for 2 weeks, what now?
A. Managers get a reminder every 3 days; after 7 days it escalates to their Director. You can also ping #finance-help.

Q. Why was my refund short by GBP 12?
A. Usually (a) over the daily meal cap, or (b) alcohol on a non-client meal. Check the rejection comments in Concur.

Q. Can I claim back stuff I bought for the team offsite?
A. Yes, if the offsite was approved in advance. Attach the approval email. Consider the corporate card next time.

Q. I paid in USD, which rate is used?
A. Concur converts at the card-statement rate if you upload the statement, otherwise the monthly Treasury rate.

Q. How soon will I see the payment?
A. Next Thursday's pay run if approved by Tuesday midnight.

Q. Lost the receipt?
A. Missing receipt declaration - max 3 per year. Beyond that the expense is yours.

Q. Do I need to claim mileage for commuting?
A. No. Commuting from home to your usual office is never reimbursable.
