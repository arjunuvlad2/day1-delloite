NEW JOINER ONBOARDING - first 90 days
Maintained by People Ops. Version 2024-06. Please report broken links in #people-ops.

BEFORE DAY 1 (hiring manager)
[ ] Order laptop via ServiceNow catalog item "New starter kit" (lead time 10 working days - do NOT wait until the Friday before!)
[ ] Raise access requests: email, Teams, Workday, Time & Expense, engagement-specific SharePoint sites
[ ] Nominate a buddy (not the manager, not someone on the same project if avoidable)
[ ] Send welcome email with first-day logistics

DAY 1
Colleague arrives 09:30 at reception, collects badge and laptop. IT hands over the device and walks through MFA enrolment - the authenticator app must be installed on the personal phone BEFORE the laptop will let you sign in. People Ops runs the 10:30 welcome session: culture, values, benefits, who's who.

WEEK 1
- Complete mandatory e-learning: Information Security Awareness (45 min), Code of Conduct (30 min), Data Privacy Essentials (30 min), Anti-bribery & Gifts (20 min). These must be done within 14 days or your access gets suspended - yes, really.
- Sign the confidentiality undertaking and the acceptable-use acknowledgement
- Meet your counselor (career coach) and agree goals
- Book time entry codes with your engagement manager
- Set up the corporate card (if eligible, after 3 months)

WEEKS 2-4
- Shadow two client calls
- Complete the "Northwind Methodology" bootcamp (2 days, virtual)
- Join your practice's community of practice meeting
- Get added to the correct distribution lists (ask buddy!)

DAYS 30-60
- 30-day check-in with line manager: expectations, early feedback, any blockers
- Start billable work at agreed utilisation (typically ramping 40% -> 70% -> target)
- Complete the "Working with clients" workshop

DAYS 60-90
- Probation review (for permanent hires, 6 months total probation but formal feedback at day 90)
- Submit first learning plan; remember the L&D budget exists (see L&D policy)
- Give feedback on onboarding to People Ops - we really do read it

COMMON GOTCHAS
 * Badge not working in lifts: ask facilities, it's a floor-access group issue not a broken card.
 * "I cant see the engagement in Time & Expense": the engagement manager needs to add you to the WBS code first.
 * Parking is not included for new joiners - waitlist applies.
