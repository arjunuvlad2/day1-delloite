Quick guide for engagement teams (Legal & Risk)

1. Our default obligation: client information stays inside the engagement team unless the contract says otherwise. Don't reuse a client's data or name in another client's proposal without written consent. Sanitise case studies (remove names, numbers, anything identifying).

2. NDAs: Northwind signs mutual NDAs on our paper where possible. Do not sign a client's NDA without Legal review if it contains: non-solicit of employees, residual-knowledge clauses missing, unlimited term, unusually broad definition of confidential information, or jurisdiction outside our usual set.

3. Information barriers: where two teams advise competing clients, use ethical walls (access group + signed acknowledgement). The Risk team keeps the register.

4. Subcontractors and freelancers need written confidentiality terms BEFORE they receive anything.

5. Returning data: at end of an engagement, return or destroy client materials as the contract says, and tick the closure checklist (see retention schedule).

6. Journalists, analysts, conference organisers: refer to Corporate Communications. Never comment on client work.
