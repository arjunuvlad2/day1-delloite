POL-SEC-001 - Information Security Policy (v6.0)
Owner: CISO | Approved by Executive Committee | Review: annually

1. SCOPE
Applies to all employees, contractors and third parties who access Northwind or client information, on any device, anywhere.

2. PRINCIPLES
Confidentiality, integrity, availability. Least privilege. Defence in depth. Security is everyone's job.

3. ACCESS CONTROL
Access is granted on a need-to-know basis via role-based groups. Managers recertify team access every quarter. Leavers' accounts are disabled on their last working day, in practice within 1 hour of the HR trigger. Privileged access requires a separate admin account and is logged.

4. DEVICES
Only company-managed devices may hold client data. Full-disk encryption, endpoint detection and automatic patching are mandatory and cannot be disabled by users. Personal devices may access email via the managed mobile app only.

5. LOSS OR THEFT
If a laptop, phone, token or paper file containing company information is lost, stolen or left on a train, tell the Security Operations Centre immediately - and in any case within one hour of noticing - on +44 20 5550 0199 or through the Report Incident button in the Self Service portal. Do not wait until you have "had a good look". Remote wipe will be triggered. Reporting quickly is never punished; concealing a loss is.

6. DATA HANDLING
Classify information as Public / Internal / Confidential (see the Data Classification Guide). Confidential data must be encrypted in transit and at rest, and shared only through approved platforms.

7. REMOTE AND PUBLIC SPACES
Use the corporate VPN on untrusted networks. Screen privacy filters are issued on request. Do not discuss client matters in public places.

8. SUPPLIERS
Third parties handling Northwind or client data must meet the same standard, contractually (see POL-PRO-003).

9. INCIDENT REPORTING
Suspected phishing, malware, misdirected emails, or unusual account activity must be reported at once; the SOC will triage within 30 minutes during business hours. Serious incidents invoke the Major Incident Process and may trigger regulatory notification within 72 hours.

10. COMPLIANCE AND SANCTIONS
Breaches may lead to disciplinary action, contract termination, and where applicable legal action. Exceptions require written approval from the CISO via the Risk Exception Register.
