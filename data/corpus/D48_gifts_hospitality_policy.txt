POL-ETH-004  Gifts & hospitality (v2.5)

Northwind has zero tolerance for bribery and corruption. Hospitality must never be given or received to influence a business decision.

RECEIVING (from clients, suppliers, bidders)
- Modest hospitality up to GBP 75 per person per occasion is OK if business-related and infrequent (not more than 3 per giver per year)
- Gifts up to GBP 50 (e.g. branded items, a bottle of wine at year-end) OK; log anything above GBP 25 in the register
- Never accept cash or cash equivalents (vouchers), or anything during an active tender
- Invitations to sporting fixtures, concerts or hospitality boxes: need line-manager pre-approval and must have a genuine business purpose, be attended by the host, and be within the limit. If it needs a flight or hotel paid by the host, decline.

GIVING
- Pre-approval from an Associate Director or above, logged in the register
- Government officials and public-sector clients: much stricter - NO gifts, hospitality needs Ethics Office approval whatever the value.
- Facilitation payments are prohibited everywhere.

Red flags: lavish, hidden, repeated, requests for favours in return, third parties asking you to route payments.

When in doubt, ask ethics@northwind-advisory.example BEFORE accepting. Declining politely is always an option: "Thank you, our policy doesn't allow us to accept."
