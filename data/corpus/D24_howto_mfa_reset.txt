HOW-TO: Re-enrol your second sign-in factor

Use this when you changed handset, factory-reset your device, uninstalled the app, or the six-digit codes are rejected.

Option A - Self-service (needs another working sign-in method or office network)
1. Go to aka.northwind-advisory.example/mfa from a trusted network (office / VPN).
2. Verify with your registered backup method (SMS to the number on file or emailed one-time link).
3. Choose "Add sign-in method" -> Authenticator app -> scan QR with the new phone.
4. Remove the old device entry.

Option B - Service Desk assisted (if you've no remaining factor)
1. Call +44 20 5550 0100 (24/7) or visit the IT Bar in any office.
2. You'll be asked to verify identity: employee number, manager's name, and a video-call check against your badge photo.
3. Desk issues a time-limited temporary access pass (valid 60 minutes). Use it to enrol the new device immediately.
Turnaround: normally under 15 minutes.

Tips
 - Migrate tokens before wiping an old phone; most authenticator apps have a transfer feature.
 - Don't pick SMS as your only backup if you travel internationally.
 - Contractors: ask your Northwind sponsor to raise the request.
