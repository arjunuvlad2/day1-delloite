POL-IT-008  End-user device lifecycle  (v2.1)

Standard cycle: laptops are replaced every 4 years (36 months for developers / data engineers on request, because builds are heavy). Phones every 3 years.

Eligibility check: Self Service > My Devices shows your asset age. Devices turn "Eligible for refresh" 90 days before the cycle date.

Early replacement criteria
- Hardware fault that the IT Bar cannot repair within 3 working days (failed screen, battery swelling, keyboard failure, liquid damage)
- Performance below the minimum spec for your role, verified by a diagnostics ticket
- Accessibility needs
Lost or stolen devices follow the Security incident route; a replacement is issued after the incident ticket is raised.

Accidental damage: first incident is free; second within 24 months, cost centre charged GBP 150 excess.

Process
1. Raise "Device Replacement" ticket in Self Service with asset tag and fault description (photo helps).
2. IT Bar offers a loaner within the same working day (stock permitting). Client-site staff: courier next business day.
3. Back up to OneDrive - devices are re-imaged, local drives are not recovered.
4. Return the old unit within 10 days; wiped and recycled via certified ITAD vendor.

Standard models: Latitude 7450 (general), ThinkPad P16 (data/analytics), MacBook Pro 14 (design/creative; business case needed).
