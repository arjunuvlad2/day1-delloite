BCP-001  Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery Plan - executive summary  (v5, annual test passed Feb 2024)

1. PURPOSE
To keep critical services running, or restore them within agreed time, when premises, technology, suppliers or people are not available. The plan covers all Northwind offices and shared services.

2. SCENARIOS COVERED
- Loss of premises (fire, water ingress, power failure, police cordon, building evacuated)
- Major technology outage (identity provider, collaboration suite, cloud region)
- Cyber attack including ransomware
- Loss of key suppliers (payroll, connectivity)
- Pandemic or travel restrictions; severe weather
- Loss of key people (succession for client-critical roles)

3. GOVERNANCE
Crisis Management Team (CMT): COO (chair), CISO, CFO, Head of People, Head of Comms, General Counsel. Convened by the on-call duty director via the emergency conference bridge. Each office has a Floor Warden and a deputy.

4. FIRST ACTIONS IF A BUILDING BECOMES UNUSABLE
a) Life safety first: evacuate and account for people at the assembly point; Floor Wardens report headcount to the CMT.
b) Floor Warden or reception notifies Facilities Helpdesk and the duty director.
c) CMT decides within 60 minutes: (i) work from home, (ii) relocate to alternate site, (iii) split teams.
d) All-staff notification by SMS, push alert and email (mass-notification tool "Northwind Alert"). Staff must keep contact details current in Workday.
e) Client-critical teams contact engagement leaders; clients are informed within 4 hours where delivery is affected.

5. RECOVERY OBJECTIVES (selected)
Service                    RTO       RPO
Email & collaboration       4 h       15 min
Time & expense / payroll    24 h      24 h
Analytics warehouse         8 h       1 h
Client portals              4 h       15 min
ERP / finance close         24 h      4 h

6. ALTERNATE WORKSPACE
Contracts with flexible-workspace providers give 150 desks in London, 60 in Manchester within 24 hours. Staff are all enabled for remote working, so most scenarios start with work-from-home.

7. TESTING
Tabletop exercise twice a year, technical DR failover once a year, call-tree test quarterly. Results go to the Executive Committee. Gaps are tracked with owners and dates.

8. COMMUNICATIONS
Use only approved channels. Don't speak to media; refer to Corporate Communications. Keep a log of decisions.

Plan owner: Head of Resilience. Full plan (restricted) held in the crisis-management SharePoint and as a printed copy in each office safe.
