POL-FIN-006  Version 4  -  Effective 1 Feb 2022

Employees can claim back reasonable business costs. Please submit claims within 60 days of incurring the cost using the Expenses Portal (legacy) or the paper form F-12 for amounts under GBP 50.

Allowed
 - Economy flights; business class if flight is over 5 hours
 - Hotels up to GBP 180/night in London, USD 300 in New York
 - Meals while on business trips: up to GBP 65 per day
 - Client entertainment: up to GBP 100 per head, manager approval
 - Taxis for late-night travel after 22:00
 - Mileage 40p/mile

Not allowed: alcohol except when entertaining clients, personal items, fines.

Receipts needed for items above GBP 20. 

Payment is made monthly with payroll (last working day).
Finance Ops, Northwind
