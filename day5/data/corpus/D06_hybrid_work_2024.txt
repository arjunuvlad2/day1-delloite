POL-HR-022  v3.0  (effective 1 April 2024)

Northwind's hybrid model: "3 + 2". Colleagues are expected to be in a Northwind office or at the client site on at least THREE days per week, and may work remotely on the other two.

- Anchor days for all teams: Tuesday and Wednesday. These are the days leadership meetings, town halls and training are scheduled.
- Third day is team-agreed (flex).
- Client-site staff: client requirement overrides; typically 4-5 days on site.
- Attendance is tracked by badge data in aggregate only. It is not used for individual performance ratings, but repeated non-compliance will be discussed by your manager.

Exceptions: documented disability adjustments, caring responsibilities (formal flexible-working request), and agreed relocations.

Home-office: GBP 250 one-off set-up allowance, claimed through the expense system.

Replaces v2.1 (2022) which required 2 days in office.
